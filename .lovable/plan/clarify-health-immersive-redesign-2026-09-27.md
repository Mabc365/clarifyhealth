# Clarify Health immersive redesign

## Goal

Give Clarify Health the confident, immersive impact of the Academy reference while remaining original, calm, accessible, and clearly focused on plain-English health education.

## What will change

- Rebuild the homepage opening as a full-width documentary-style health scene with a bold, immediate message and one primary action.
- Turn the header into a compact floating navigation surface with the existing logo, Topics, Tools, About, language, and account actions.
- Restyle the existing homepage sections into spacious editorial bands: three trust points, three-step process, one example article, mobile-app announcement, and final action.
- Simplify the footer into three clear link groups while retaining medical and legal disclaimers.
- Refine mobile navigation and section layouts so the same hierarchy works on small screens.

## Visual direction

- **Palette:** Ocean Signal — deep teal, pale blue-gray, near-white, and coral reserved for the main action.
- **Typography:** confident sans-serif display treatment with highly readable supporting text; multilingual fonts remain supported.
- **Imagery:** one original, human documentary-style health image in the opening; no stock-style medical clichés.
- **Motion:** subtle image drift and restrained content reveals, disabled for reduced-motion users.
- **Reference use:** borrow the Academy site's immediacy, floating navigation, strong scale, and single-action discipline—not its branding, copy, or exact composition.

## Technical details

- Update homepage, header, footer, global tokens/styles, and font loading.
- Use semantic theme colors and preserve dark mode, high contrast, keyboard focus, translation, and authentication behavior.
- Keep every existing route and feature unchanged.
- Verify desktop and mobile layouts, navigation, contrast, reduced motion, and the current build status.
