# Match The Academy’s layout system more closely

## Goal
Refine Clarify Health beyond a similar palette. Recreate the reference site’s defining composition—oversized immersive opening, compact two-tier floating navigation, restrained typography, long editorial spacing, structured grids, image-led modules, and strong light/dark section transitions—while keeping Clarify Health original, accessible, and medically appropriate.

## Homepage
- Replace the current left-aligned opening with a full-viewport image scene, centered statement, and one compact coral action.
- Rebuild the header as a wider two-tier glass panel: brand and account action above, primary links in a divided row below; collapse it cleanly on mobile.
- Add the reference’s quiet introductory interlude after the opening: generous off-white space with one centered Clarify Health statement.
- Turn the three trust points into a precise editorial grid with restrained labels, compact copy, and subtle borders.
- Present “How it works” as a large showcase module with a simple visual sequence instead of a generic dark split section.
- Make the article preview more image-led and editorial, followed by the mobile-app section and one final high-contrast action.
- Keep the page intentionally sparse and preserve one primary action per section.

## Shared visual system
- Use off-white surfaces, near-black/teal text, coral actions, thin rules, 8px corners, wide margins, and a compact mono-style label treatment.
- Standardize headings to a clean modern grotesk feel and remove remaining old Playfair/Fraunces/DM Sans styling from the visible core pages.
- Use large vertical intervals and deliberate full-width bands rather than floating cards.
- Preserve reduced-motion behavior, keyboard focus, contrast, RTL languages, and the OpenDyslexic option.

## Core pages
- Apply the same page-opening rhythm, editorial rules, list/grid treatment, typography, and CTA hierarchy to Topics, Tools, and About.
- Keep every route, translation, authentication state, tool, and data behavior unchanged.

## Verification
- Check homepage, Topics, Tools, and About at desktop and mobile widths.
- Verify navigation, language selection, sign-in/sign-up states, RTL layout, and all primary links.
- Confirm no overlap, clipping, console errors, or build errors.
